About

HealthyLife is a small health info website with easy guides and calculators.

Why this website?

Most health info online is either too technical or someone is trying to sell a product. Here the idea is to keep it simple and only share what organisations like WHO and CDC actually recommend.

The calculators also show how the result is calculated so you know where the number comes from.

What we follow

  • Every guide has its sources listed at the bottom
  • Simple language, no difficult medical words
  • Nothing you type in the tools is saved

What we don't do

  • No selling medicines, supplements or diet plans
  • No "cure" claims
  • We are not doctors, so this is not a replacement for one

Let us know on the contact page.
