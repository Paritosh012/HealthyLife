Sleep Exercise Water Health numbers

4 min read

How much sleep do adults need?

Adults need 7 hours or more of sleep per night. Teenagers need 8-10 hours. Sleeping less than this for a long time is linked to weight gain, BP, diabetes and low mood.

Why a fixed routine helps

The body has an internal clock. If you sleep and wake up at the same time daily (weekends included), it gets easier to fall asleep and wake up.

Signs you're not sleeping enough

  • Hard to get out of bed in the morning
  • Feeling sleepy in class or office
  • Needing chai/coffee to get through the afternoon

Some tips

  • Get some sunlight in the morning
  • Avoid tea and coffee in the evening
  • Keep the phone away for the last hour
  • Dark, quiet and cool room

See a doctor if you snore loudly and stop breathing for a few seconds while sleeping, can't sleep properly for weeks, or feel sleepy while driving.

How much exercise is enough?

According to WHO, adults should do 150-300 minutes of moderate exercise per week, or 75-150 minutes of intense exercise. Plus muscle strengthening 2 days a week.

Moderate vs intense

  • Moderate - you can talk but can't sing. Brisk walking, slow cycling.
  • Intense - you can only say a few words before taking a breath. Running, football.

Short sessions also count

You don't have to do it all at once. Three 10 minute walks in a day also count, so do stairs and walking to the market.

Sit less

Sitting for long hours is bad even if you exercise. Get up and move around every half hour or so.

Check with a doctor first if you have a heart condition or get chest pain/dizziness while exercising.

How much water should you drink?

Total water needed per day is around 3.7 litres for men and 2.7 litres for women, and this includes water from food. About 80% comes from drinks, so roughly 3 L for men and 2.2 L for women.

Other drinks count too

Tea, milk, juice all count. Plain water is still best since there's no sugar in it.

How to check

  • Drink when you feel thirsty
  • Light yellow urine usually means you're drinking enough
  • Drink more in summer and after exercise

Heart or kidney patients should follow their doctor's advice, some people are told to drink less.

Health report numbers

Common numbers you'll see in a health checkup report. One reading doesn't mean much on its own, always show it to a doctor.

BMI

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) x height (m)

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 - 24.9Normal
25 - 29.9Overweight
30+Obese

For Indians and other South Asians the risk starts going up from around 23.

Blood pressure

Written as two numbers like 118/76. Below 120/80 is normal. Doctors measure it a few times before calling it high BP.

Blood sugar

TestNormalPrediabetesDiabetes
Fasting sugarBelow 100 mg/dL100 - 125 mg/dL126 mg/dL or more
HbA1cBelow 5.7%5.7 - 6.4%6.5% or more

Show your report to a doctor. They'll look at age, family history and other tests too.
