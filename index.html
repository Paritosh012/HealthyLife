3 habits for better sleep

Our body works on a roughly 24 hour clock. Light and routine keep it in sync.

Morning sunlight

Step outside after waking up. Daylight tells the body that the day has started.

Fixed wake-up time

Try to wake up around the same time every day, weekends too.

No phone before bed

Screen light late at night makes it harder to fall asleep.
